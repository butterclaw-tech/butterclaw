# ButterClaw Governance

## Overview
ButterClaw is an open-source security harness for agentic AI. As a Growth-stage project, our governance is designed to be lightweight, transparent, and focused on rapid iteration while maintaining security integrity.

## The Maintainer Role
Currently, ButterClaw is led by its founder (Lead Maintainer). The Lead Maintainer is responsible for:
- Strategic direction and project roadmap.
- Final approval on all Pull Requests (PRs).
- Management of the project's security policy.

## Decision Making
We operate on a **Lazy Consensus** model. Major changes are proposed via GitHub Issues or Discussions. If no objections are raised within 72 hours, the proposal is considered accepted. 

## Becoming a Maintainer
We welcome contributors to grow into maintainer roles. Users who consistently contribute high-quality code, documentation, or security research over a 3-month period can be nominated for Maintainer status by the Lead Maintainer.

## Alignment
ButterClaw aligns with the **Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)** mission to create secure, interoperable, and transparent agentic infrastructure.
