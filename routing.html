🛡️ Deterministic Guardrails — Policy Engine (v0.6.1)

The Policy Engine constrains the probabilistic Brain with deterministic rules. Pre-brain filters short-circuit known patterns without burning inference time. Post-brain validators can override, escalate, or require higher confidence. Pre-tool gates block specific MCP tool calls in chains. Policies are evaluated in priority order (lowest number first). First non-"allow" match wins.

🔔 Alert Dispatcher — External Notification Engine (v0.6.2)

Pushes notifications to external channels (webhook, Discord, ntfy, email, Gotify) when critical events occur. Rules map event types to channels with configurable cooldowns. Webhooks are HMAC-SHA256 signed. Gibson alerts fire BEFORE vault destruction — the notification always escapes. Channel secrets are stored outside the ButterVault by design.
